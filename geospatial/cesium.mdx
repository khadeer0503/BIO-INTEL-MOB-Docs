# Geospatial integration

The Unity application uses **Cesium for Unity** to stream geographically positioned city content, including Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles accessed through Cesium ion where configured.

## Coordinate handling

- Treat latitude, longitude, and ellipsoidal height as geographical inputs, not raw Unity world positions.
- Convert to world-local coordinates through the active `CesiumGeoreference`.
- Recalculate/ground proposal markers after terrain or tiles become available.
- Treat georeference changes during city switching as a spatial lifecycle event.

## Rendering and physics

3D Tiles visualization and collision-ready object geometry are **different concerns**. A visual building/road surface may not provide suitable collision geometry or a stable placement mesh. Street inspection should use curated colliders and a deliberately bounded reference surface where appropriate.

## Compliance check

Before embedding Street View imagery, caching derived content, or exporting tiles/assets, verify licensing, attribution, and usage restrictions for the actual Google/Cesium contracts.
