# Introduction

BIO-INTEL-MOB uses a multi-city, immersive approach to explore urban mobility concerns and assess potential interventions. Its VR application places geolocated proposals into a streamed geospatial environment.

## Primary workflows

1. Choose a pilot site and explore its city environment in overview mode.
2. Discover geolocated proposals and inspect their metadata.
3. Enter a bounded street-inspection workspace.
4. Preview, place, and edit configurable urban objects.
5. Trigger traffic/pedestrian simulations where a SUMO service is configured.

## Documentation principles

- Distinguish **implemented**, **prototype**, and **planned** behavior.
- Do not present assumed WordPress response schemas as deployed contracts.
- Separate end-user workflows from infrastructure and developer guides.
- Reference source code, tagged releases, and screenshots when detailed behavior is added.
- Review third-party data terms and licensing before sharing restricted visuals or assets.
